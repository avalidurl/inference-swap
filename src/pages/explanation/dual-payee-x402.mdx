# Dual-payee x402

CURB is **live on x402**. A match payment is not a single transfer. The 402 challenge lists **two** accepts:

1. **Seller** — USDC to `sellerPayTo` for the matched slice
2. **Hub** — protocol fee to `HUB_PAY_TO` (bps + floor)

## Rails

- **USDC on Base** (`eip155:8453`)
- **USDC on Solana** (SPL via x402)
- Buyers can pay via **[pay.sh](https://pay.sh/)** on Solana — buyer-side discovery/CLI. Settlement remains x402 USDC; pay.sh is **not** a seller acceptor.

Buyers (or their wallets / facilitators / pay.sh clients) must satisfy the challenge before the hub marks the match paid and issues a grant.

Why dual: seller gets paid for capacity; protocol fee funds the rail without the hub custodying inference keys or proxying token streams.
