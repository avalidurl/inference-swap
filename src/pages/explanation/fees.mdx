## Hub swap fees (x402)

Protocol fee is taken as a **second x402 accept** on match pay (not skimmed from seller settlement off-band).

Defaults (env):

- `FEE_BPS` — basis points of the matched price (e.g. `500` = 5%)
- `MIN_FEE_USDC` — floor (e.g. `0.001`)
- `HUB_PAY_TO` — fee recipient (Base)
- `HUB_PAY_TO_SOLANA` — fee recipient (Solana)
- Asset: **USDC** via x402 on **Base** and **Solana**

We accept payment in USDC on x402 on both Base and Solana, and via pay.sh on Solana. That does not change the dual-payee split.

Exact live values: `GET https://api.curb.numetal.xyz/.well-known/x402` and the 402 body on `POST /v1/matches/{id}/pay`.
