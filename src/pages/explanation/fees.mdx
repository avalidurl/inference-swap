# Fees

Protocol fee is taken as a **second x402 accept** on match pay (not skimmed from seller settlement off-band).

Defaults (Base / env):

- `FEE_BPS` — basis points of the matched price (e.g. `500` = 5%)
- `MIN_FEE_USDC` — floor (e.g. `0.001`)
- `HUB_PAY_TO` — fee recipient
- Asset: USDC on Base (`eip155:8453`)

Exact live values: `GET https://curb.numetal.xyz/.well-known/x402` and the 402 body on `POST /v1/matches/{id}/pay`.
